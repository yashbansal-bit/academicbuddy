MADE FOR CURIOUS MINDS

Our Mission

AcademicBuddy helps students research carefully, understand complex information, and keep schoolwork organised in one calm space.

Schoolwork can ask you to do several things at once: choose a question, find evidence, make sense of what you read, and remember what is due next. AcademicBuddy brings those steps into one student-friendly workspace so it feels easier to decide what to do next.

We believe good research starts with curiosity and grows through careful questions. Our tools are designed to help students explore evidence, build confidence, and make their own informed decisions—not replace their thinking.

▤

Reliable Sources

Discover research records, inspect the original work, and compare evidence. A search result is only a starting point, so we encourage you to check who created it, what it supports, and whether it fits your question.

▣

Clear Explanations

Break unfamiliar ideas into manageable steps, ask follow-up questions, and practise with examples while keeping the important details and your own voice.

♙

Designed for Students

Bring research, study support, deadlines, and personal progress into one welcoming workspace. Your calendar and grades help you stay organised without pretending to replace your school tools.

A GENTLER WAY TO GET STARTED

Big assignments, broken into smaller steps

Good learning is rarely one perfect answer. It is a series of small choices: ask a better question, look at the evidence, make a connection, and choose one useful next step.

Start with curiosity

Search helps you turn a broad topic into a question you can investigate. Explore author and publication details, follow promising leads, and use your teacher’s criteria when deciding what belongs in your work.

Make ideas clearer

Chat Buddy can explain a concept, offer a hint, or help you plan a study session. Use its response to guide your own thinking, check important claims against the linked reading suggestions, and keep your own voice in every assignment.

Keep your next step visible

Calendar tasks and grade records give you a simple place to notice deadlines and progress. They are private to your account, entered by you, and are not automatically linked to a school platform.

✳

HELP THAT RESPECTS YOUR WORK

A study companion, not a shortcut

AcademicBuddy is here to make the process of learning feel more manageable—not to do your thinking for you. Source records can be incomplete, suggested references may not answer your exact question, and AI can make mistakes. Open the original sources, check the evidence, follow your teacher’s rules, and ask a trusted adult when something important is unclear.

YOUR STUDY SPACE, STEP BY STEP

How To Use AcademicBuddy

Start with a question, explore the evidence, and keep track of what you learn. Each tool is here to support your work—not do your assignment for you.

You do not need to use every tool at once. Begin with the part of your schoolwork that feels unclear, then choose the next small action that will help you move forward.

01

Search with a question

Try a focused topic or key terms. Use research records to discover titles, authors, and publishers you can investigate. If results are too broad, add a specific place, period, or subject phrase.

Search sources →
02

Check the source

Read the original work. Check who wrote it, what evidence it uses, when it was published, and whether it fits your question. Compare its claims with other credible work before you rely on it.

Ask Chat Buddy
